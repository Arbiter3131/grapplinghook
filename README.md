Controls:

WASD-movement

LMB- Hold LMB to charge a teleport forward, release to teleport (has a max distance and sends you the max velocity in the direction you were looking)

RMB- Grapple to a surface within range.

Space- Jump, and when near a wall in mid-air, press space to wallrun, with space jumping you off of the wall.
